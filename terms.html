What you are buying

ProposalPilot is a hosted, done-for-you service. We configure and run a proposal-sending tool for you on our server, which you use through your own private link, and which sends from your own Gmail account. The monthly fee covers running the service, configuration changes, and support. There is no setup fee and nothing is installed on your computer.

Setup

Setup begins once you have provided the details we ask for: your business information, pricing, packages, and branding, plus authorising the Gmail connection. We aim to have you sending within two business days of receiving everything.

Fees, minimum term, and cancellation

  • The fee is $99 per month, billed in advance, exclusive of VAT where VAT applies.
  • The first three months are a minimum term. This covers the setup work, which we do before you have paid for it.
  • After the minimum term, you can cancel at any time with no notice period. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; we do not pro-rate partial months.

What happens when you stop paying

This is a subscription to a running service, not a one-time purchase of software, so it is worth being explicit: when your subscription ends, your access ends. Your ProposalPilot stops working at the end of the period you have paid for, and you can no longer send proposals through it.

Before that happens:

  • If a payment fails, we do not cut anything off immediately. We contact you and allow at least seven days to fix it.
  • When your subscription ends, we send you a PDF archive of every proposal and contract you have sent, so your records stay with you.
  • Your Gmail account, your sent mail, and your client relationships are unaffected — they were always yours, and copies of everything you sent remain in your own Sent folder.

Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for keeping your Gmail account and its credentials secure.
  • You must not use the tool to send unsolicited bulk email, or for anything unlawful.

Contract templates are not legal advice

The contract documents generated by the tool are templates. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed for your jurisdiction or your particular situation. Have a lawyer review them before relying on them.
