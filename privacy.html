Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

ProposalPilot is operated by an independent sole trader based in Finland. This page explains what data we collect and what we do with it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email addresses. If you submit the waitlist form, we receive your email address through Formspree, our form-handling provider.
  • Customer details. If you buy a plan, we collect the business information needed to configure your tool: your name, business name, contact email, service packages and pricing, and branding assets.
  • Correspondence. Emails you send us and our replies.

This website uses no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies.

What we do with it

We use your data only to contact you about ProposalPilot, to configure and support your installation, and to handle billing. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Your proposals and your Gmail

The ProposalPilot tool runs on your own computer and connects to your Gmail account through Google's official API, using credentials that stay on your machine. Proposals, contracts, client details, and sent messages are stored on your device and in your own Google account. We do not receive copies of them and we cannot read your mailbox.

Service providers

  • Formspree — receives and forwards waitlist form submissions.
  • Google — hosts the fonts used by this page and provides the Gmail API used by the tool.
  • Stripe — processes payments, if you buy a plan. We never see your full card details.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to remove them or until the waitlist closes. Customer records are kept for as long as you are a customer, plus the period required by Finnish accounting law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email lehtonen.akseli1@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
