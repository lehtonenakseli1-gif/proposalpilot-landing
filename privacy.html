Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

ProposalPilot is operated by an independent sole trader based in Finland. This page explains what data we collect and what we do with it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details. If you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, a description of what you sell, and anything else you choose to write. This reaches us through Formspree, our form-handling provider.
  • Customer details. If you become a customer, we collect the business information needed to run your ProposalPilot: your name, business name, contact details, service packages and pricing, and branding assets.
  • The contents of your ProposalPilot. Client names, contact details, and the proposals and contracts you generate are stored on our server as part of running the service for you.
  • Gmail access token. Stored encrypted, so the service can send on your behalf.
  • Correspondence. Emails you send us and our replies.

This website uses no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies.

What we do with it

We use your data only to run the service for you, to contact you about it, and to handle billing. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

Where the service runs

ProposalPilot is a hosted service. It runs on a server in the European Union, and you use it through your own private link — your data is kept on a separate instance and is not mixed with other customers' data.

Your clients' details and the proposals you generate are therefore stored by us, and we are a data processor for that information. Copies of everything you send also remain in your own Gmail Sent folder.

Your Gmail

The service connects to your Gmail through Google's official API. We request one permission only — gmail.send, which allows sending mail as you. We cannot read, search, or delete your email, and we do not have your Google password. You can revoke our access at any time from your Google account's security settings, which stops the service from sending.

Service providers

  • Formspree — receives and forwards contact form submissions.
  • Google — hosts the fonts used by this page and provides the Gmail API used by the service.
  • Our hosting provider — runs the server, located in the EU.
  • Stripe — processes payments. We never see your full card details.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept until you ask us to remove them. Customer data is kept for as long as you are a customer. When your subscription ends we send you a PDF archive of your proposals and contracts, and delete your instance and its data within 30 days of the end of your paid period — sooner if you ask. Invoicing records are kept for the period required by Finnish accounting law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email lehtonen.akseli1@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
